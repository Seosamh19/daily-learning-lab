## 📜 Poem of the Day

**"Answer to Some Elegant Verses Sent by a Friend to the Author, Complaining That One of His Descriptions Was Rather Too Warmly Drawn"** — *George Gordon, Lord Byron*

>   "But if any old Lady, Knight, Priest, or Physician,
>    Should condemn me for printing a second edition;
>    If good Madam Squintum my work should abuse,
>    May I venture to give her a smack of my muse?"
> 
>    Anstey's 'New Bath Guide', p. 169.
> 
> Candour compels me, BECHER! to commend
> The verse, which blends the censor with the friend;
> Your strong yet just reproof extorts applause
> From me, the heedless and imprudent cause;
> For this wild error, which pervades my strain,
> I sue for pardon,--must I sue in vain?
> The wise sometimes from Wisdom's ways depart;
> Can youth then hush the dictates of the heart?
> Precepts of prudence curb, but can't controul,
> ...
