## 📜 Poem of the Day

> Corpses are cold in the tomb;
> Stones on the pavement are dumb;
> Abortions are dead in the womb,
> And their mothers look pale--like the death-white shore
> Of Albion, free no more.

> Her sons are as stones in the way--
