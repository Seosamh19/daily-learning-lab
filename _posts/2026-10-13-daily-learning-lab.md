## 📜 Poem of the Day

**"Fragment: 'Ye Gentle Visitations of Calm Thought'"** — *Percy Bysshe Shelley*

> Ye gentle visitations of calm thought--
> Moods like the memories of happier earth,
> Which come arrayed in thoughts of little worth,
